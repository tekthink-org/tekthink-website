The Problem

Indian builders are executing blind.

Real estate and infrastructure projects in India lose 20–40% of their value through execution failures — not bad design, not wrong location. Execution blindness. Construction, sales, finance, and customer experience running in disconnected silos with no system connecting them.

⚠️
No real-time execution visibility
Progress reports arrive two days late in five formats. By the time the MD knows Tower B is 44 days behind, possession dates are already at risk and RERA penalties are accumulating.
📊
EVM exists only in theory
CPI and SPI are compiled manually from Tally and site books — quarterly, not daily. Cost overruns are discovered after handover, not predicted during execution when correction is still possible.
🏗️
Construction not connected to revenue
A plastering delay on Floor 9 impacts 23 possession dates, ₹1.2Cr RERA penalty exposure, and 23 buyer escalations. No existing tool connects these automatically — a human must trace each impact manually.
👤
Buyers call for every update
The buyer has no visibility into their flat's progress, payment schedule, or RERA compliance status. Every query is a phone call. NPS collapses before possession — not because of quality, but because of silence.
📋
RERA as a quarterly scramble
Quarterly progress reports are assembled manually from site books, Tally entries, and email threads. One missed filing triggers a penalty. No builder in Tier 2–4 India has a system that auto-assembles RERA reports.
💸
Cashflow crises arrive without warning
Construction milestones that trigger payment demands are not connected to actual site progress or bank feeds. Shortfalls materialise 30 days after they could have been predicted and averted.

How It Works

Seven-step intelligence loop, running continuously.

Every project, every day — the iQube intelligence loop captures, computes, connects, alerts, advises, automates, and learns. From raw site input to MD-level advisory in real time.

⬆ Capture→ ✓ Compute→ 🔗 Connect→ ⚡ Alert→ 📋 Advise→ 📤 Automate→ 📈 Learn
01
Capture — three data ingestion methods
Manual entry by staff. System feeds via APIs from Tally, Salesforce, MS Project, WhatsApp, and RBI Account Aggregator. AI automation via NLP and rules engine. Computed values — EVM, progress %, NPS — are derived outputs, never entered manually.
02
Compute — binary completion drives all metrics
Progress % is never entered subjectively. Engineers mark sub-tasks as Completed or In Progress. The system multiplies status by pre-defined weightages from the 4-project-type standard task master (303 sub-tasks). Unit % rolls up automatically to floor, tower, project, portfolio, and RERA.
03
Connect — iQube cross-dimensional reasoning
One sub-task completion simultaneously updates: unit heatmap, EVM earned value, buyer portal progress, RERA quarterly %, cashflow milestone trigger. No manual connection between systems. No reconciliation required.
04
Alert — iQube Decision Intelligence engine
The proprietary engine fires cross-dimensional alerts when signals converge across iQube axes. Alerts are specific: owner, action, deadline, financial impact. Advisory mode in v1.0 — no hard blocks without human confirmation.
05
Advise — specific, actionable, owned
Not generic dashboards. ProjectIQ tells the MD: "Approve night shift for Tower B — saves 30 days at ₹4.2L. Decision needed today." Every advisory has an owner, a deadline, and a predicted outcome.
06 & 07
Automate & Learn — compounds with every cycle
Payment demand notes auto-drafted on milestone completion. RERA reports auto-assembled 30 days before deadline. Every project back-tests predictions against actuals. The iQube engine learns which delay patterns produce which downstream impacts — improving alert precision with every cycle.
